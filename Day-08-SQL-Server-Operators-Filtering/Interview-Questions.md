# Day 08 — Interview Questions

Q49. What is SELECT?

Answer: SELECT is used to display data from a table.

Q50. What is WHERE?

Answer: WHERE is used to apply filter conditions and retrieve specific rows.

Q51. What are Arithmetic Operators?

Answer: Arithmetic operators are +, -, *, / and %.

Q52. What are Relational Operators?

Answer: Relational operators are <, >, <=, >=, =, <> and !=.

Q53. What are Logical Operators?

Answer: Logical operators are AND, OR and NOT.

Q54. What is a compound condition?

Answer: Multiple conditions combined using AND or OR are called a compound condition.

Q55. Which has higher priority, AND or OR?

Answer: AND has higher priority than OR.

Q56. How can condition priority be controlled?

Answer: Parentheses ( ) can be used to control condition priority.

Q57. When is a row selected by WHERE?

Answer: A row is selected when the WHERE condition is TRUE.

Q58. How can passed students be filtered?

Answer: Students are passed when S1, S2 and S3 are all greater than or equal to 35.

Q59. How can failed students be filtered?

Answer: Students are failed when any one subject is less than 35.
