# Day 09 — Interview Questions

## Q1. What is the IN operator?

**Answer:** IN is used to compare a column with multiple values.

---

## Q2. What is NOT IN?

**Answer:** NOT IN is used to exclude multiple values from the result.

---

## Q3. What is the BETWEEN operator?

**Answer:** BETWEEN is used to compare a value within a specified range.

---

## Q4. What is NOT BETWEEN?

**Answer:** NOT BETWEEN is used to exclude values within a specified range.

---

## Q5. What is the difference between IN and BETWEEN?

**Answer:** IN is used for list comparison, while BETWEEN is used for range comparison.

---

## Q6. What is the equivalent condition of BETWEEN?

**Answer:**

```sql
BETWEEN 5000 AND 10000
